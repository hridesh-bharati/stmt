Frequently Asked Questions

The following documents are required: (1) Student's birth certificate, (2) Previous school's mark sheet and transfer certificate, (3) Copy of Aadhar card, (4) 4 passport-size photos, (5) Copy of parent's Aadhar card.

Our fees are affordable for all families. Pre-Primary (Nursery-UKG): ₹800/month, Primary (Grades 1-5): ₹1000/month, Secondary (Grades 6-10): ₹1200/month. Annual charges, exam fees, and development fund are separate.

Yes, the school has its own buses. Transport is available for villages within 10 km of Nichlaul, including Sonauli, Farenda, Dhani, Bargadwa, Kotwa, etc. Bus fee is ₹300/month extra.
